What is CavBot Arcade?

CavBot Arcade is the game surface inside CavBot. You can open it from the platform, choose a game, and play anytime from a supported device.

Are all games available to play?

Yes. All six Arcade games are available to play inside CavBot Arcade at all times, no matter which plan you use.

What is the difference between Arcade and 404 games?

Arcade is where you play the games inside CavBot. 404 games are the installable website version used when a visitor lands on a missing page.

How many 404 games can I install?

Only one Arcade game can be active on your 404 page at a time. Your plan controls how many games you can choose from: Free includes 1 option, Premium includes 3 options, and Premium+ unlocks all 6.

Do 404 games replace fixing broken links?

No. A 404 game makes the broken moment better for the visitor, but the route should still be repaired, redirected, or restored when needed.

How should a 404 game be installed?

Install it through the approved CavBot runtime or CDN loader. After launch, check that the game loads, the return links work, and the recovery context is recorded.
